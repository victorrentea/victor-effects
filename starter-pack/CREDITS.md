# Starter pack — credits

Every sound and tile picture in this folder may be redistributed. The
machine-readable ledger is `credits.json`; `tools/check-starter-pack.py` refuses
a release with an uncredited file.

- **Sounds**: CC0 recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), public-domain
  recordings from Wikimedia Commons, and five synthesized for this project. Cut,
  faded and loudness-normalized here.
- **Tile pictures**: [Noto Emoji](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji) (Copyright 2013 Google LLC,
  Apache License 2.0) placed on a coloured background.
